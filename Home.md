## A small daily rhythm

1. **Start:** check deadlines and follow-ups; pick one useful outcome.
2. **Interrupt:** capture who asked, the source link, the next action, and any promised date.
3. **Switch:** record what is done, what comes next, and who is blocking you.
4. **Close:** record one outcome with evidence. Review unprocessed requests.
