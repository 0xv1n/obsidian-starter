# Annual reviews

[[Home]] · [[90 System/Templates/Annual Review|Annual review template]]

Create a note here named for the year, such as `2026`, then insert **Annual Review**. The year in the filename drives its evidence queries, including when reviewing an earlier year.

The review gathers daily outcome bullets, completed personal tasks, and completed projects. Use that evidence to describe your contribution and impact, including the small requests that rarely make it into Jira.
